Brief implementation

BaselineWhat we implemented
Caption IterRAG 2BSplit video into 30 s clips, generate grounded captions with Qwen3-VL-2B, embed captions/questions with Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B, retrieve from FAISS for at most three query-rewrite rounds, then answer from caption text with Qwen3-VL-2B.
Caption-FAISS 9BUse the same caption index and iterative retrieval loop, but answer from the retrieved caption strings with Qwen3.5-9B and thinking disabled.
Visual-FAISS 9BEncode video clips directly with Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B, retrieve the top visible clips under the timestamp cutoff, and let Qwen3.5-9B answer from the retrieved video pixels rather than captions.
DispiderMaterialize only the visible-prefix video, then run the released Dispider checkpoint end to end with its official streaming compressor and CLIP vision tower.
PROXYM3-AgentBuild a causal bank of visible clips, select a small top-k clip set with the local retrieval proxy, and answer with stock Qwen3.5-9B. We do not use the official M3 Memorization or Control checkpoints.
PROXYStreamBridgeApply StreamBridge's released round-decayed embedding-buffer policy, which compresses older visual rounds while retaining text memory, then answer at known timestamps with stock Qwen3.5-9B. We do not use activation or Stream-IT weights.

All runners enforce the visible-time cutoff before retrieval or inference. The flat StreamingBench aggregate includes SQA/Proactive rows and is not a full free-running proactive reproduction.
